# Blugold Lavarand

Our attempt at [Lavarand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lavarand) is *very early* in the idea phase and we don't have many details worked out quite yet. Reed's preliminary thoughts on this idea are detailed below. 

## Logical Idea

The idea is to point a camera at a collection of devices (see [hardware](#hardware) below) and use the camera feed's data as a source of entropy for the campus.

## Hardware

We are considering multiple options of devices, lava lamps (as seen in [the original](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/bb/Lava_lamp_wall_at_Cloudflare_office_-2.jpg/960px-Lava_lamp_wall_at_Cloudflare_office_-2.jpg)), clocks, fish tank, etcetera.
